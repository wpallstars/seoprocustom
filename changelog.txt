== Changelog ==

Every change to SEO Pro Custom. readme.txt lists the latest version in short.

= Unreleased =
* Development files only, no change to the plugin's behaviour: core files synced from WP Plugin Starter 1.0.60, including the secret text setting, the hooks check (scripts/check-hooks.js, skipped until docs/HOOKS.md exists), scripts/release-bump.sh, the changelog check, minified assets in release builds (SEOProCustom::asset_url()), the deploy load-check retry and the auto-dispatch label on the Starter sync issue (GH#18).

= 0.1.2 =
* Development files only, no change to the plugin's code: the smoke test's customisation checks, the issue chooser's contact links and the DCO sign-off step in CONTRIBUTING.md are kept by the Starter sync.

= 0.1.1 =
* Settings screen: the Customisations card has inner padding and space above it, a line saying where your changes go with a link to the Read Me tab, and it replaces the "No settings yet" placeholder on the General tab.
* README: fork the plugin, install your fork on the sites you want to customise and reuse it across them; keep your fork in sync; issues and pull requests here are for the blank plugin itself. New banner strapline.
* Licence headers on the example customisation; the plugin's own .gitignore lines are kept by the Starter sync.

= 0.1.0 =
* First version, made from WP Plugin Starter 1.0.56.
* Customisation loader for `customizations/*.php` (Description, Sites, Requires, Upstream and Retire-When headers, optional .css and .js), with PHP errors reported and skipped.
* Loaded, skipped, retired and failed states in Site Health, on the settings screen and in `wp seoprocustom list`.
* Product request issue form and a DCO check for pull requests.
